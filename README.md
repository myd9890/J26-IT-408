# Edge AI Framework for Banana Disease Management

An **Edge AI-based framework** for proactive disease management in Cavendish banana plantations in Sri Lanka. The project integrates computer vision, few-shot learning, environmental sensing, multimodal AI, explainable AI, and resource optimization to support early disease detection and informed plantation management.

## Research Components

### 1. Few-Shot Disease Detection and Explainable AI

Develops a disease detection system capable of identifying banana diseases using limited labelled images. Few-shot learning supports the detection of rare, emerging, and early-stage diseases, while explainable AI provides interpretable evidence for model predictions.

### 2. Environmental Disease Risk Prediction

Predicts disease risk before visible symptoms appear by analysing environmental factors such as temperature, humidity, rainfall, soil moisture, and leaf wetness.

### 3. Disease Progression and Severity Forecasting

Combines disease images, environmental conditions, temporal information, and severity indicators to estimate current disease severity and forecast potential disease progression.

### 4. Severity-Based Resource Optimization

Uses predicted disease risk, severity, and progression to prioritize labour, treatments, field operations, and other available resources toward areas requiring greater attention.

## Objective

The overall objective is to shift banana disease management from a reactive, symptom-based approach to a proactive, risk-aware, and severity-based strategy. The framework aims to enable earlier intervention, improve disease monitoring, and support efficient resource utilization through integrated Edge AI technologies.
